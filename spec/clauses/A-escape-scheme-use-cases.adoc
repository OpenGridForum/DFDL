== Appendix A: Escape Scheme Use Cases

This appendix gives additional examples of escape schemes and how they affect
interpretation of (parsing) or creation of (unparsing) the data stream.

The tables can be interpreted as the physical data column on the right is parsed
to obtain the logical data on the left, or the logical data on the left is
unparsed to create the physical representation on the right.
